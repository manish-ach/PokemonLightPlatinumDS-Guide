What they evolve into

The gift is the first stage. Everything downstream is also absent from the regional dex, so the only way to a Klinklang or a Greninja is to evolve the gift.

  • Klink→ Level 38 Klang→ Level 49 Klinklang
  • Porygon→ Trade holding Up Grade Porygon2→ Trade holding Dubious Disc Porygon-Z
  • Chespin→ Level 16 Quilladin→ Level 36 Chesnaught
  • Fennekin→ Level 16 Braixen→ Level 36 Delphox
  • Froakie→ Level 16 Frogadier→ Level 36 Greninja
  • Beldum→ Level 20 Metang→ Level 45 Metagross
