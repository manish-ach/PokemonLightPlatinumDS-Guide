Secret Tunnel

Line 1 rotate and Line 2 move-outside are linked underground. Either one stays usable even if the ladder picks it as the blocked lever.

How to play
  1. Click an empty intersection to drop a gear, or click an existing gear to remove it. Drag a gear to slide it to a new intersection.
  2. Pick a target line from the pill row and pick which lever the ladder blocks.
  3. Press a lever button at the rim or press Solve to let the solver compute the lever order.
  4. Rotate moves red-track gears clockwise and green-track gears anticlockwise. Outside / Inside step every gear on that line by one track.
  5. The puzzle is solved when the target line holds four gears, one on each track.
