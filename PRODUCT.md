# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Players of the Pokémon Light Platinum DS ROM hack, mid-game, usually with the DS
in one hand. They arrive with a specific question — what lives on Route 404, which
badge unlocks Fly, where the Sun Palace levers go — and want the answer without
reading around it. Many are on a phone beside the console.

## Product Purpose

A field guide to a game that quietly stops behaving like the HeartGold it is built
on. It exists because the hack's own text, tables and encounter lists are the only
trustworthy source and nobody else has read them out. Success is a player finding
the fact they came for in one scan, trusting it because it is sourced, and leaving.

## Brand Personality

Reliquary. Precise, sourced, unhurried. The voice states what the cartridge says
and says plainly when it does not know. Three words: exact, quiet, earned. Gold is
ornament for what is verified, never decoration for its own sake.

## Anti-references

- Fan-wiki sprawl: walls of unsourced prose, rumour presented as fact.
- Marketing-page rhythm on reference surfaces: hero sections, eyebrows, progress
  theatre ("7 of 15 visited") where no progress is being tracked.
- Reading layouts for lookup tasks: a 57-item index squeezed into a prose column.
- Decorative states: chips, thumbnails and "done" markers that carry no meaning.

## Design Principles

1. **The row is the answer.** A place's row should contain what the player came
   for; no click to reveal what could have been shown.
2. **Say only what the name doesn't.** "Yellow Town · Town" is one word too many.
3. **Density is respect.** Dense, aligned, scannable beats airy and pretty on a
   lookup page. Save air for the chapters.
4. **Empty is a sentence, not a row.** Undocumented places are listed in one quiet
   line, never as a run of blank rows.
5. **Sourced or silent.** If the game doesn't state it, the guide doesn't either.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Body text never lighter than `--ink-quiet` (4.8:1 floor, documented in tokens).
Gold is never used for text in light theme. Reduced motion honoured on every
transition. Touch targets ≥44px on interactive rows; the site is used on phones.
